      How ranking works
      
    
        Five tiers of three divisions, 100 RP each. Past Diamond III every 100 RP is a Legend star.
        Wins earn less and losses cost more as you climb.

        Each calendar month (UTC) is a season. When a new one starts you drop one tier from the start
        of your division, and the season you finished is kept as a medal. Badges, stats and unlocked
        tables and card backs are never reset.
